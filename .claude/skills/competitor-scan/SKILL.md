# Competitor Scan

The user wants a structured read on the competitive field — same dimensions
for every competitor, so differences are visible instead of anecdotal.

## Method

1. **Fix N and the dimensions first.** Use the competitors the user named;
   if they gave a number without names, pick the N most relevant and say
   why those. Choose 4-6 comparison dimensions that matter for the user's
   decision (e.g. positioning, pricing, target customer, distribution,
   momentum, weaknesses) — the same set for all.
2. **Same questions for everyone.** Fill every cell from sources, not vibes.
   Cite as you go; mark cells you couldn't verify as unknown rather than
   guessing.
3. **Momentum, not just position.** Where evidence allows, note direction —
   growing, stalling, pivoting — and what signal shows it.
4. **End with the so-what.** A table without a read is homework, not
   analysis.

## Output

- **Executive summary** — one line: the shape of the field and where the
  opening is.
- **Comparison table** — competitors × dimensions, every filled cell
  sourced, unknowns marked.
- **Per-competitor note** — 2-3 sentences each: their real strength, their
  real weakness, their momentum.
- **The opening** — where the field is weak and what that implies for the
  user, stated as hypothesis with the evidence that supports it.
- **Source list** — with links.
